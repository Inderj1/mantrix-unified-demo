Customer CLV Segments
Fader segmentation — forward-looking CLV, not backward-looking revenue. Computed from purchase history patterns in VBAK + margin in ACDOCA.
P(Alive) Survival Curves — sBG Model
Probability a customer is still "active" as a function of time since last purchase. Computed from VBAK order frequency + recency patterns. Higher α = more loyal cohort.
sBG Survival Function (Fader & Hardie)
P(alive at period t | α, β) = B(α, β + t) / B(α, β)
where B = Beta function, α/β = shape params fitted to VBAK purchase cadence
Input: VBAK-ERDAT (order dates) per VBAK-KUNNR (customer) over trailing 24 months
Champions (α=5, β=1.2)
Loyal (α=3, β=2)
At-Risk (α=1.5, β=4)
New Accounts (α=1, β=8)
CLV Rank vs. Revenue Rank

CLV₁₂ = P(Alive) × E[X(12)] × Avg_Margin_per_Order  |  E[X(t)] = BG/NBD predicted future transactions  |  All inputs: VBAK (frequency/recency), ACDOCA (margin), BSID (payment), KNVV (segment)
Expected Future Purchases — BG/NBD Model
12-month purchase probability distribution per segment. Inputs: x (frequency), t_x (recency), T (observation window) from VBAK.
BG/NBD Expected Transactions (Fader & Hardie 2005)
E[X(t) | r,α,s,β,T,x] = (r+x)/(α+T) × A(r+x+1, α+T, s, β+T)
r,α = transaction heterogeneity params · s,β = lifetime heterogeneity params
x = VBAK order count · t_x = days since last VBAK-ERDAT · T = 24-month window
